What You Will Observe in This Library

This video archive covers practical application of Speech & Language Videos, demonstrating techniques tested with neurodiverse children. Rather than pure theory, you observe our clinicians and Dr. Aniqa Sohail illustrating cues, reinforcement timings, physical prompts, and emotional attunement.

  • Direct 1:1 therapist-to-child intervention sessions
  • How to prompt without triggering resistance or meltdowns
  • Pacing, visual cues, and positive reinforcer delivery
  • Translating centre exercises into daily home routines
