What You'll Typically Find in This Library

This video archive covers real, practical implementation of Safety Training Videos, demonstrating techniques tested with neurodiverse children. Rather than pure theory, you observe our clinicians and Dr. Aniqa Sohail illustrating cues, reinforcement timings, physical prompts, and emotional attunement.

  • Direct 1:1 therapist-to-child intervention sessions
  • How to prompt without triggering resistance or meltdowns
  • Pacing, visual cues, and positive reinforcer delivery
  • Translating centre exercises into daily home routines
