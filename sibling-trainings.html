Sibling Support & Integration

Course Overview & Learning Outcomes

Dedicated empathetic groups helping brothers and sisters of autistic children understand neurodiversity with joy and pride.

Led by Dr. Aniqa Sohail and senior multidisciplinary clinical staff, this curriculum bridges international theoretical science with hands-on application in Pakistani family dynamics and educational ecosystems.

Core Modules & Practicum

  • Age-appropriate explanations of autism and sensory differences
  • Fun reciprocal games siblings can play comfortably at home
  • Safe space to express feelings of frustration, worry, or embarrassment
  • Celebrating neurodiversity and building lifelong brotherly/sisterly bonds

Logistics, Cohort Schedule & Eligibility

Audience: Siblings (Ages 6 to 18)

Duration: Monthly Weekend Sessions

Location: HELP Autism Pakistan Training Hall, Model Town Extension, Lahore (Hybrid virtual sessions available for overseas parents and distant cities).
